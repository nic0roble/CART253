# Final Reflections  

## November 21, 2026

I'm having a tough time deciding what my final project is going to be. We were told that we should maybe create a game, and I thought that could be a good idea. So, I went to OpenProcessing to look for inspiration and see what other people had created. That's when I came across *The Last Lovely Day* by megamitts, a small game where a farmer has to water his flowers before a meteor strikes.

I really liked the idea, so I think I want to create something similar. I also want to use bouncing animations in my code because, in megamitts' game, they made everything look more alive. I'm going to need to figure out how she did that. I also want to make my project feel more like an actual game. Megamitts used closed shapes for almost everything, and her farmer looked more alive because she also animated his legs.

I think using her project as a reference will help me make sure mine doesn't look too plain or boring at first glance.
